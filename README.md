## Visualizations Created

### 1. Distribution of Movies and TV Shows

**Question:** What type of content dominates Netflix's library?

**Visualization:** Bar Chart

**Insight:** Movies significantly outnumber TV Shows, indicating that Netflix focuses heavily on movie content while maintaining a smaller collection of TV series.

---

### 2. Top 10 Content Producing Countries

**Question:** Which countries contribute the most content to Netflix?

**Visualization:** Horizontal Bar Chart

**Insight:** The United States contributes the highest number of titles, followed by India and the United Kingdom. This highlights Netflix's strong presence in major entertainment markets.

---

### 3. Top 10 Netflix Genres

**Question:** Which genres are most common on Netflix?

**Visualization:** Horizontal Bar Chart

**Insight:** Dramas, International Movies, and Comedies are among the most popular genres, reflecting diverse audience preferences across regions.

---

### 4. Netflix Rating Distribution

**Question:** What audience categories does Netflix primarily target?

**Visualization:** Count Plot

**Insight:** TV-MA is the most common rating, indicating that a large portion of Netflix content is targeted toward mature audiences.

---

### 5. Content Release Trend Over Years

**Question:** How has content production changed over time?

**Visualization:** Line Chart

**Insight:** The number of released titles has generally increased over time, demonstrating growth in content production and content availability.

---

### 6. Netflix Content Growth by Year Added

**Question:** How rapidly has Netflix expanded its content library?

**Visualization:** Line Chart

**Insight:** Netflix experienced rapid growth after 2015, with a significant increase in content additions to the platform.

---

### 7. Missing Values Heatmap

**Question:** Does the dataset contain missing information?

**Visualization:** Heatmap

**Insight:** Several columns contain missing values, particularly Director, Cast, and Country, indicating areas requiring data cleaning and preprocessing.

---

### 8. Correlation Heatmap

**Question:** Are there relationships among numerical variables?

**Visualization:** Correlation Heatmap

**Insight:** The dataset contains limited numerical variables, resulting in minimal correlations, but the visualization provides an overview of variable relationships.

---

## Key Findings

- Movies dominate Netflix's content catalog.
- The United States is the leading contributor of Netflix content.
- India is one of the major content-producing countries.
- Dramas, International Movies, and Comedies are the most common genres.
- TV-MA is the most frequent content rating.
- Netflix experienced substantial content growth after 2015.
- Missing values exist in several important columns.
- Visualizations successfully revealed patterns and trends hidden within raw data.

## Data Story

The visualizations collectively tell the story of Netflix's growth as a global streaming platform.

The analysis shows that Netflix's catalog is heavily dominated by Movies. The United States remains the largest contributor of content, while countries such as India play an important role in content production. Genre analysis reveals strong popularity for Dramas, International Movies, and Comedies. Audience analysis shows a major focus on mature viewers through TV-MA-rated content.

The growth visualizations further demonstrate Netflix's rapid expansion after 2015, reflecting increased investment in content acquisition and original productions.

Together, these visualizations transform raw data into meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.
